How the value is computed

ln(market cap) = %%VALUE_CONST%% + %%VALUE_SLOPE%% × ln(TVL) + category effect, fitted by ridge regression (λ=8 on the category dummies) on 95 traded protocols with a positive market cap and a listing date. Category effects are shrunk toward zero; a category with no traded peer gets an effect of zero. Launch value = exp of the fitted value at the protocol’s TVL and category. Token odds are a logistic fit on 645 protocols with a listing date: logit = %%ODDS_RAW_CONST%% + %%ODDS_RAW_YEARS%% × ln(years listed) + %%ODDS_RAW_SHARE%% × category token share (shrunk, k=32) + %%ODDS_RAW_TVL%% × ln(TVL) + %%ODDS_RAW_GROWTH%% × 7-day growth (winsorized). Protocols with no listing date use the median years of no-token protocols (%%MEDIAN_YEARS%%). Implied value = launch value × odds.

Deposit 0.02% of TVL. The airdrop pool is 7% of the implied value; the farmer’s share is pro-rata to the deposit’s share of TVL. The implied value already carries the launch odds, so they are not applied a second time. Costs are gas summed from the ten steps ($78) plus 0.05% of the deposit in entry/exit fees. Work is 4.5 hours plus 15 minutes a week for 12 weeks (7.5 hours). Farm needs net above the T-bill return on the deposit (4.5% a year over 12 weeks) AND above $25 an hour. Marginal is a positive net that fails a test; Skip is a net at or below zero.

Without a token means both gecko_id and symbol are null — market cap alone misclassifies 258 protocols. Excluded: CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC, versioned names, wrapped / staked / staking products, exchange-prefixed names. A protocol records no token; the snapshot never proves it has none.

Live peers are the 95 protocols whose token trades with a recorded market cap and a listing date; they fit the value model. 128 protocols record a token with a usable market cap. The board never ranks a protocol whose token trades.

Limits, stated plainly. Spearman rank correlation of leave-one-out predictions is %%SPEARMAN%%, worse than v1’s 0.405: the model prices the typical token better (median miss ×2.93 vs ×3.31) but orders similar tokens slightly worse. The odds walk-forward is weaker than base (see above). The snapshot is one day, 2026-11-14; nothing here predicts when a token launches, only what it would be worth if the record stayed as it is.
